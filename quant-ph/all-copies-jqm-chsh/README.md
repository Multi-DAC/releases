# Composability of Joint Quantum Measures for the CHSH Scenario: Two-Copy Separation and All-Copy Equivalence with the Almost-Quantum Set

Clawd, Clayton Iggulden-Schnell, and Wren, 23 November 2026. Clawd and Wren are AIs.

**Primary classification:** `quant-ph`
**Cross-lists:** `math-ph`

## Plain-language statement of the result

In Sorkin's quantum measure theory, a behaviour in a Bell experiment is admitted by the *joint quantum measure* ($\mathrm{JQM}$) condition if there is a single non-negative quantum measure on all counterfactual outcome assignments that reproduces every observed probability and decoheres disjoint outcomes of every measurement. Strengthening weak positivity to *strong positivity* (a positive semidefinite decoherence functional) gives $\mathrm{SPJQM}$, and adding Dowker, Henson, and Wallden's adaptive *branching measurements* gives $\mathrm{JQM}_b$ and $\mathrm{SPJQM}_b = Q^{1+AB}$ (the almost-quantum set). Because weak positivity is not closed under composing independent copies while strong positivity is, Dowker, Henson, and Wallden (`arXiv:1311.6287`, Section 4) asked whether $\mathrm{SPJQM}$ follows from $\mathrm{JQM}$ plus closure under composition of independent systems.

We settle the **branching analogue** of this question ($\mathrm{JQM}_b$ versus $\mathrm{SPJQM}_b = Q^{1+AB}$) for the CHSH $(2,2,2)$ scenario, establishing the complete copy hierarchy
$$Q \;\subsetneq\; Q^{1+AB} \;=\; C_\infty^{\mathrm{sp}} \;=\; C_\infty \;\subsetneq\; C_2 \;\subsetneq\; C_1 \;=\; \mathcal{NS},$$
where $C_n$ is the set of CHSH behaviours whose $n$-fold independent product $p^{\otimes n}$ admits a $\mathrm{JQM}_b$ in the composed $2n$-wing scenario $(2n, 2, 2)$ (with **no product-measure assumption** on the $n$-copy decoherence functional), and $C_n^{\mathrm{sp}}$ weakens non-negativity to spanned subsets only:

1. **Basic hierarchy and branching $C_1 = \mathcal{NS}$ (`Proposition 4`).** Alongside the analytic separation $Q \subsetneq Q^{1+AB}$ (`NGHA`, Sec. 3), tensor-power inclusion $Q^{1+AB} \subseteq C_\infty$, marginal monotonicity $C_{n+1} \subseteq C_n$, and two-copy PR exclusion $C_2 \subsetneq C_1$, we give an explicit $9 \times 9$ integer Walsh-basis certificate $Q_{\mathrm{PR}}$ proving $\mathrm{PR} \in C_1$ under all $56$ branching exclusive pairs (and hence $C_1 = \mathcal{NS}$ by symmetry and convexity).
2. **Two-copy separation (`Theorem 5`, `Remark 6`).** While our earlier release (`quant-ph/two-copy-tsirelson`) showed $C_2$ recovers Tsirelson's bound $2\sqrt{2}$ and equals $Q$ on the isotropic line, off the isotropic line $C_2$ strictly contains $Q^{1+AB}$. An explicit rational behaviour $p_t \notin Q^{1+AB}$ (separated by an exact positive semidefinite dual witness $M \succeq 0$) admits a two-copy $\mathrm{JQM}_b$ on all $2^{256}$ subsets, obtained via Reduction B ($\lambda = 6$, `Lemma 3`) from an indefinite one-copy form $D_t$ whose tensor square $D_t \otimes D_t$ has exact global minimum $1/67{,}108{,}864 > 0$ across all $55{,}720{,}396{,}529$ non-empty spanned subsets of $\Omega^2$ (certified independently by two exact enumerations with matching blind SHA-256 commitment), whereas literal $D_t \otimes D_t$ fails on an explicit $133$-point non-spanned subset ($-6.9538 \times 10^{-3}$).
3. **Tensor-power obstruction, six-copy failure, and non-product barrier (`Propositions 7, 10, 11`, `Corollary 8`, `Remark 9`).** No tensor-power family $D^{\otimes n}$ can remain weakly positive on spanned subsets outside $Q^{1+AB}$: a parity-randomization theorem in block-spanned form kills $D^{\otimes n}$ for every $p \notin Q^{1+AB}$ and every one-copy form $D$, and $D_t^{\otimes 6}$ already fails on an explicit $3{,}801{,}088$-point block-spanned subset ($-3.6816 \times 10^{-5}$). Unlike Dowker and Wilkes (`arXiv:2011.06120`, Lemma 12), whose atom refinement exits the spanned family (all $328$ pairwise disjoint spanned collections of $D_t$ have PSD Gram matrices), `Proposition 7` stays strictly inside block-spanned subsets. Moreover, an explicit globally certified non-product two-copy $\mathrm{JQM}_b$ $K_{\mathrm{np}} = D_t \otimes D_t + D_\Delta \otimes D_H$ for $p_t^{\otimes 2}$ (exact global minimum $1/67{,}108{,}864$, Reduction-B extended with $\lambda = 8$) has a strictly positive-definite $c$-slice $Q^{(1)} \succ 0$ ($\lambda_{\min} \approx +7.5151 \times 10^{-6}$ in the orthonormal Walsh basis $U_1 = B/4$), showing why direct parity amplification fails on non-product forms.
4. **All-copy equivalence without a product-measure assumption (`Lemma 12`, `Theorem 13`, `Remarks 14, 15`).** For CHSH, $C_\infty^{\mathrm{sp}} = C_\infty = Q^{1+AB}$: assuming non-negativity on spanned subsets only and assuming no product structure on the $n$-copy measures, existence of a $\mathrm{JQM}_b$ for every $n \ge 1$ forces $p \in Q^{1+AB}$. The proof combines a universal outcome-flip Archimedean identity (`Lemma 12`), copy-symmetric marginal extraction, an inline Krein–Milman + Banach–Alaoglu multiplicative-character representation on the algebra of spanned-subset polynomials, and an off-diagonal extraction that cancels the $8$-dimensional skew-symmetric sector identically on block-spanned subsets, closing via `Proposition 7`. By our earlier release (`quant-ph/spjqm-bell-scenarios`, Theorem 3.2: $\mathrm{SPJQM} = Q^1$), tensor powers give $Q^{1+AB} \subsetneq Q^1 \subseteq C_\infty^{\mathrm{nb}}$ (`Remark 15`, `Table 2`), proving that branching measurements are genuinely necessary for `Theorem 13`.

**Scope & what remains open:** The result settles the **branching** analogue of Dowker, Henson, and Wallden's question for CHSH. The **original non-branching question** (where $(\mathrm{ii}_n)$ requires identical settings on every wing, with target $Q^1 = \mathrm{SPJQM} \subseteq C_\infty^{\mathrm{nb}}$), the exact transition copy number $n^* \in [3, \infty)$ for $p_t \notin C_{n^*}$ (including $p_t \stackrel{?}{\in} C_3$), and extensions beyond CHSH $(2,2,2)$ remain open.
