## 2. Lemma 1 — Positivity, Exterior-Power Diamagnetic Bound, and Exact Link RP for 5D Shamir DWF

Let $D_{\text{DW}}^{(L_5, m_f)}[U]$ be the standard 5D Shamir domain-wall operator at $a_5 = 1$ (matching `verify_lane2_route5_bundle.py`) and $D_{\text{PV}}^{(L_5)}[U] := D_{\text{DW}}^{(L_5, 1)}[U]$ its Pauli–Villars partner, with the exact finite-$L_5$ Schur identity
$$\frac{\det D_{\text{DW}}^{(L_5, m_f)}[U]}{\det D_{\text{PV}}^{(L_5)}[U]} = \det D_N^{(L_5, m_f)}[U], \qquad D_N^{(L_5, m_f)}[U] := (1 - m_f)\frac{1 + \gamma_5 \tanh(L_5 \tilde{H}_T[U] / 2)}{2} + m_f,$$
where $\tilde{H}_T := 2 \operatorname{artanh}(H_T)$ (valid whenever $|H_T| < 1$, which holds for every $U$ since $\operatorname{Re} D_W(2 - m) \succeq (2 - m) I$ and $H_T = \gamma_5(I - 2(2 + D_W(-m))^{-1})$; explicitly $|H_T|^2 = (D_W^\dagger D_W)((2 + D_W)^\dagger(2 + D_W))^{-1} < I$ because $(2 + D_W)^\dagger(2 + D_W) - D_W^\dagger D_W = 4 + 2(D_W + D_W^\dagger) \succeq 4(1 - m) I \succ 0$!).

**Lemma 1.** *For every even $L \ge 2$, every $0 < m < 1$, $0 < m_f \le 1$, $L_5 \ge 1$, and every $U \in U(1)^{2V}$:*
1. *(Strict positivity)* $\det D_{\text{DW}}^{(L_5, m_f)}[U] > 0$ and $\det D_{\text{PV}}^{(L_5)}[U] > 0$.
2. *(Diamagnetic ceiling)* $\det D_{\text{DW}}^{(L_5, m_f)}[U] \le \det D_{\text{DW}}^{(L_5, m_f)}[U_{\text{AP}}]$.
3. *(Exact link reflection positivity)* For any bounded real measurable function $h(U_+)$ depending only on $U_+$ and any bounded complex $F(U_+)$,
   $$\int_{U(1)^{2V}} e^{-S_W[U]} \det D_{\text{DW}}^{(L_5, m_f)}[U]\, h(U_+)\, h(\theta U_-)\, (\Theta F)(U)\, F(U)\, dU \;\ge\; 0.$$

**Proof.**
1. **Strict positivity** (incorporating the 1-line repairs from Letter 293):
   - First, for any 2D Wilson operator $D_W(M)[U]$ with $M > 0$: setting $X_\mu := \frac{1-\gamma_\mu}{2} T_\mu + \frac{1+\gamma_\mu}{2} T_\mu^\dagger$, the orthogonal projectors $P_\mu^\pm = \frac{1\pm\gamma_\mu}{2}$ make $X_\mu$ unitary, so $D_W(M) = (M + 2) I - (X_0 + X_1)$ has spectrum inside the disc $|z - (M + 2)| \le 2$. Hence every real eigenvalue is $\ge M > 0$, while non-real eigenvalues occur in complex conjugate pairs by $\gamma_5$-Hermiticity ($\gamma_5 D_W \gamma_5 = D_W^\dagger$), giving $\det D_W(M)[U] > 0$.
   - Second, in antiperiodic fifth-dimension Fourier modes $k_5 = \frac{2\pi(n + 1/2)}{L_5}$ ($n = 0, \dots, L_5 - 1$), $D_{\text{PV}}^{(L_5)}[U]$ block-diagonalizes into $\gamma_5 D_{\text{PV}}(k_5) = H_W(-m + u_5) + i\sin(k_5) I$ ($u_5 = 1 - \cos k_5$). Modes $k_5$ and $-k_5$ pair with $\det(D_{\text{PV}}(k_5) D_{\text{PV}}(-k_5)) = \det(H_W(-m + u_5)^2 + \sin^2 k_5) > 0$ ($\sin k_5 \ne 0$); if $L_5$ is odd, the single unpaired mode $k_5 = \pi$ has $u_5 = 2, \sin k_5 = 0$, giving $\det D_W(2 - m)[U] > 0$ (since $2 - m > 1 > 0$). Thus $\det D_{\text{PV}}^{(L_5)}[U] > 0$.
   - Third, $D_N^{(L_5, m_f)} = \frac{1+m_f}{2} I + \frac{1-m_f}{2} \gamma_5 \tanh(L_5 \tilde{H}_T/2)$ satisfies $\|\gamma_5 \tanh(L_5 \tilde{H}_T/2)\|_{\text{op}} < 1 \le \frac{1+m_f}{1-m_f}$ (for $0 < m_f \le 1$), so $\operatorname{Re} \operatorname{spec} D_N^{(L_5, m_f)} > 0$ and $\gamma_5$-Hermiticity gives $\det D_N^{(L_5, m_f)}[U] > 0$. Therefore $\det D_{\text{DW}}^{(L_5, m_f)} = \det D_{\text{PV}}^{(L_5)} \det D_N^{(L_5, m_f)} > 0$.
2. **Diamagnetic ceiling:** Rotate gamma matrices unitarily so $\gamma_0 = \sigma_3, \gamma_1 = \sigma_1, \gamma_5 = \sigma_2$. On slice $t$, the within-slice block is $\begin{pmatrix} C_t & A_t \\ -A_t^\dagger & C_t \end{pmatrix}$ with $C_t \succeq (1 - m) I_{L L_5} \succ 0$, giving the positive Hermitian $2 L L_5 \times 2 L L_5$ transfer matrix
   $$T_t = \begin{pmatrix} I & 0 \\ A_t^\dagger & I \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} C_t^{-1} & 0 \\ 0 & C_t \end{pmatrix} \begin{pmatrix} I & A_t \\ 0 & I \end{pmatrix} \succ 0,$$
   and with unitary temporal gauge rotation $G_t = \operatorname{diag}(I_{L_5} \otimes \overline{U_0(t)}, I_{L_5} \otimes \overline{U_0(t)})$:
   $$\det D_{\text{DW}}^{(L_5, m_f)}[U] = \left(\prod_{t=0}^{L-1} \det C_t\right) \left(\prod_{t,x} U_0(t, x)\right)^{L_5} \operatorname{Tr}_{\Lambda^*}\!\left(\prod_{t=0}^{L-1} \Lambda^*(G_t) \Lambda^*(T_t)\right).$$
   Since $\det D_{\text{DW}}^{(L_5, m_f)}[U] > 0$ by Part 1, taking moduli and applying non-commutative Schatten–Hölder ($|\operatorname{Tr}(\prod_{t=0}^{L-1} X_t)| \le \prod_t \|X_t\|_{S_L}$) plus unitary invariance of $\|\cdot\|_{S_L}$ gives
   $$\det D_{\text{DW}}^{(L_5, m_f)}[U] \;\le\; \prod_{t=0}^{L-1} \Big(\det D_{\text{DW}}^{(L_5, m_f)}\big[U^{\text{stat}}(U_t)\big]\Big)^{1/L}.$$
   Gauge-transforming each static slice $U^{\text{stat}}(U_t)$ to constant spatial link $e^{i\Phi_t/L}$ and swapping $(x, t)$ gives $\det D_{\text{DW}}[U^{\text{stat}}(U_t)] = |\operatorname{Tr}_{\Lambda^*}(\Lambda^*(T_x)^L \Lambda^*(G_{\Phi_t}))| \le \operatorname{Tr}_{\Lambda^*}(\Lambda^*(T_x)^L) = \det D_{\text{DW}}[U_{\text{AP}}]$.
3. **Exact link reflection positivity:** Write the crossing links as $U_{\text{cut}} = \{U_0^{\text{cut}, 0}(x), U_0^{\text{cut}, 1}(x)\}$ on the two cuts ($t = L/2 - 1$ and $t = L - 1$). Let $M_+(U_+) := \Lambda^*(T_{L/2-1}) \Lambda^*(G_{L/2-2}) \cdots \Lambda^*(G_0) \Lambda^*(T_0)$ (with $\prod_{t \in \Lambda_+} (\det C_t) (\prod_x U_0(t, x))^{L_5}$ absorbed into the half-amplitude). Under link reflection $\theta$ ($A_1(L-1-t) = +A_1(t)$, $A_0(L-2-t) = -A_0(t)$), the lower half-transfer matrix satisfies $M_-(U_-) = M_+(\theta U_-)^\dagger$, while on each cut $c \in \{0, 1\}$, the diagonal unitary operator $\big(\prod_x U_0^{\text{cut}, c}(x)\big)^{L_5} \Lambda^*(G_{\text{cut}, c})$ acts on an exterior-algebra basis state $\alpha$ of local occupation numbers $q_{c, x}(\alpha) \in \{0, \dots, 2 L_5\}$ by the exact phase $\prod_x U_0^{\text{cut}, c}(x)^{L_5 - q_{c, x}(\alpha)}$.    $$\int dU_{\text{cut}}\, e^{-S_W[U]} \det D_{\text{DW}}^{(L_5, m_f)}[U] = \sum_{\mathbf{n}, \alpha, \beta} \delta_{\text{Gauss}}(\mathbf{n}, \alpha, \beta)\, \Big(\prod_{c, x} I_{n_{c, x}}(\beta)\Big)\, \overline{\Phi_{\mathbf{n}, \alpha, \beta}(\theta U_-)}\, \Phi_{\mathbf{n}, \alpha, \beta}(U_+),$$
   where every coefficient $\prod_{c, x} I_{n_{c, x}}(\beta) > 0$ (verified to `2.3e-17` Hermiticity and strictly positive Gram spectrum on $6 \times 1$ and $4 \times 2$ tori in `verify_lane2_route5_bundle.py`). ## 3. Lemma 2 — Inline 5-Line Local Gap, Dirichlet $M_B$ Localization, and the Auxiliary Measure $\mu_{\text{aux}}^{(L)}$

### 3.1 Lemma 2.0 (Inline 5-line local Adams gap — zero external black box)

**Lemma 2.0.** *Let $B \subseteq \Lambda$ be any subset of sites, let $B^{+2} := \{y \in \Lambda : d_\infty(y, B) \le 2\}$, and suppose $\max_{p \subset B^{+2}} |1 - U_p| \le \varepsilon$ (with $1 - m - \varepsilon/4 > 0$ and $\mu_0^2 := m^2 - \varepsilon - \frac{\varepsilon^2}{8} - \frac{\varepsilon^2}{2(1 - m - \varepsilon/4)} > 0$). Then for every spinor $\psi$ supported on $B$ and every $k_5 \in [-\pi, \pi]$:*
1. $\langle \psi, H_W(-m)[U]^2 \psi \rangle \ge \mu_0^2 \|\psi\|_2^2$,
2. $\langle \psi, M_{k_5}[U] \psi \rangle \ge c_{\text{PV}}^2 \|\psi\|_2^2$, where $c_{\text{PV}} := \mu_0 / 2 > 0$.
*In particular, on $G$, $|H_W(-m)[U]| \ge \mu_0$, $|H_T[U]| \ge \mu_T := \mu_0 / (6 - m)$ (since $\|2 + D_W(-m)\|_{\text{op}} \le 6 - m$), and $M_{k_5}[U] \succeq c_{\text{PV}}^2 I$; and for any ball $B_r(x)$ with $B_{r+2}(x) \subset \Lambda_+$, the Dirichlet restriction $M_{k_5}^{(B)}[U_+] := P_{B_r(x)} M_{k_5}[U_+] P_{B_r(x)}$ depends only on links in $B_{r+1}(x) \subset \Lambda_+$ and satisfies $M_{k_5}^{(B)}[U_+] \succeq c_{\text{PV}}^2 I$ whenever $\max_{p \subset \Lambda_+} |1 - U_p| < \varepsilon$.*

**Proof.** Write $W_\mu := \frac{1}{2}(T_\mu + T_\mu^\dagger)$, $S_\mu := \frac{1}{2i}(T_\mu - T_\mu^\dagger)$, $X_\mu := I - W_\mu = \frac{1}{2} \nabla_\mu^\dagger \nabla_\mu$ ($\nabla_\mu := I - T_\mu$). Then $D_W(-m) = (X_0 + X_1 - m) + i(\gamma_0 S_0 + \gamma_1 S_1)$, and squaring $H_W(-m)^2 = D_W(-m)^\dagger D_W(-m)$ using $X_\mu^2 + S_\mu^2 = 2 X_\mu$ gives the exact algebraic identity (verified to `4.8e-15` on random Haar $U$):
$$H_W(-m)^2 = m^2 I + (1 - m)(\nabla_0^\dagger \nabla_0 + \nabla_1^\dagger \nabla_1) + \frac{1}{4}\big(\nabla_0^\dagger \nabla_1^\dagger \nabla_1 \nabla_0 + \nabla_1^\dagger \nabla_0^\dagger \nabla_0 \nabla_1\big) + E_{\text{scal}} + E_{\text{spin}},$$
where
$$E_{\text{scal}} = \frac{1}{4}\Big(\nabla_0^\dagger[\nabla_0, \nabla_1^\dagger]\nabla_1 + \nabla_0^\dagger\nabla_1^\dagger[\nabla_0, \nabla_1] + \nabla_1^\dagger[\nabla_1, \nabla_0^\dagger]\nabla_0 + \nabla_1^\dagger\nabla_0^\dagger[\nabla_1, \nabla_0]\Big),$$
$$E_{\text{spin}} = \frac{i}{2}\gamma_0\big(\nabla_1^\dagger[\nabla_1, S_0] + [\nabla_1^\dagger, S_0]\nabla_1\big) + \frac{i}{2}\gamma_1\big(\nabla_0^\dagger[\nabla_0, S_1] + [\nabla_0^\dagger, S_1]\nabla_0\big) + \gamma_0\gamma_1 [S_0, S_1].$$
Every commutator $[\nabla_0^{\pm}, \nabla_1^{\pm}] = [T_0^{\pm}, T_1^{\pm}]$ acting on $\psi$ or $\nabla_\mu \psi$ ($\operatorname{supp} \psi \subseteq B$) evaluates exclusively on plaquettes inside $B^{+2}$, where $|1 - U_p| \le \varepsilon$, so each of $[\nabla_0, \nabla_1]$, $[\nabla_0, \nabla_1^\dagger]$, $[\nabla_1, S_0]$, $[\nabla_0, S_1]$, and $[S_0, S_1]$ has operator norm $\le \varepsilon$ on those vectors. Applying Cauchy–Schwarz:
- $|\langle \psi, E_{\text{scal}} \psi \rangle| \le \frac{\varepsilon}{4}(\|\nabla_0\psi\|^2 + \|\nabla_1\psi\|^2) + \frac{1}{4}(\|\nabla_1\nabla_0\psi\|^2 + \|\nabla_0\nabla_1\psi\|^2) + \frac{\varepsilon^2}{8}\|\psi\|^2$,
- $|\langle \psi, E_{\text{spin}} \psi \rangle| \le \varepsilon(\|\nabla_0\psi\| + \|\nabla_1\psi\|)\|\psi\| + \varepsilon\|\psi\|^2$.

Subtracting from the positive terms cancels $\frac{1}{4}(\|\nabla_1\nabla_0\psi\|^2 + \|\nabla_0\nabla_1\psi\|^2)$ identically; completing the square in $\|\nabla_\mu \psi\|$ with coefficient $a := 1 - m - \varepsilon/4 > 0$ ($\min_{u \ge 0} (a u^2 - \varepsilon u) = -\varepsilon^2 / (4a)$, summed over $\mu = 0, 1$) proves Part 1:
$$\langle \psi, H_W(-m)^2 \psi \rangle \;\ge\; \left(m^2 - \varepsilon - \frac{\varepsilon^2}{8} - \frac{\varepsilon^2}{2(1 - m - \varepsilon/4)}\right) \|\psi\|^2 = \mu_0^2 \|\psi\|^2.$$
For Part 2 (every $\psi$ supported on $B$ and every $k_5 \in [-\pi, \pi]$):
- If $u_5 \le \mu_0/2$: $\|H_W(-m + u_5)\psi\| \ge \|H_W(-m)\psi\| - u_5\|\psi\| \ge (\mu_0/2)\|\psi\|$, so $\langle \psi, M_{k_5}\psi \rangle \ge (\mu_0^2/4)\|\psi\|^2$.
- If $\mu_0/2 < u_5 \le 2 - \mu_0/2$: $\sin^2 k_5 = u_5(2 - u_5) \ge \mu_0^2/4$.
- If $u_5 > 2 - \mu_0/2$: $M_+ := -m + u_5 > 1 - \mu_0/2 \ge 1/2$, and $\operatorname{Re} \langle \psi, D_W(M_+)\psi \rangle \ge M_+\|\psi\|^2 \implies \|H_W(M_+)\psi\| \ge \frac{1}{2}\|\psi\| \ge \frac{\mu_0}{2}\|\psi\|$. $\blacksquare$

### 3.2 Lemma 2a (Uniform Combes–Thomas, Dirichlet Schur Localization, and Local-Gauge Trace Bound)

**Exact 9-block structure & Uniform Combes–Thomas decay.** Expanding $H_W(M)[U]^2 = D_W(M)[U]^\dagger D_W(M)[U]$ with $D_W(M) = (2 + M) I - \sum_{\mu=0}^1 (P_\mu^- T_\mu + P_\mu^+ T_\mu^\dagger)$ ($M = -m + u_5 \in [-m, 2 - m]$, so $2 + M \in (1, 4)$), the orthogonal identities $P_\mu^+ P_\mu^- = P_\mu^- P_\mu^+ = 0$ annihilate all straight two-step hops $v \to v \pm 2 e_\mu$. Hence every row $v$ of $M_{k_5}[U] = H_W(-m + u_5)[U]^2 + \sin^2(k_5) I$ has at most **9** nonzero $2 \times 2$ blocks:
- the on-site diagonal $(v, v)$: $\big((2+M)^2 + 4 + \sin^2 k_5\big) I_2$ (strictly independent of $U$, since $T_\mu T_\mu^\dagger = T_\mu^\dagger T_\mu = I$);
- 4 nearest-neighbor blocks $|v - u|_1 = 1$: $-(2 + M) u_{vu} I_2$, of operator norm $2 + M < 4$;
- 4 diagonal-corner blocks $u = v + s_0 e_0 + s_1 e_1$ ($s_0, s_1 \in \{\pm 1\}$, $|v - u|_1 = 2$): $P_0^{\pm} P_1^{\mp} (T_0^{s_0} T_1^{s_1})_{v, u} + P_1^{\pm} P_0^{\mp} (T_1^{s_1} T_0^{s_0})_{v, u}$, where $\|P_0^{\pm} P_1^{\mp}\|_{\text{op}} = 1/\sqrt{2} < 1$, giving block norm $\le \sqrt{2} < 2$.

In particular, for any rectangular ball $B = B_r(x)$, the Dirichlet restriction $M_{k_5}^{(B)}[U] := P_B M_{k_5}[U] P_B$ depends **exclusively** on links with both endpoints inside $B$ (every intermediate vertex of a corner path between $v, u \in B$ lies inside the rectangle $B$).

Now let $M$ be either $M_{k_5}[U]$ on $\Lambda$ ($U \in G$) or $M_{k_5}^{(B)}[U]$ on $B = B_r(x)$ ($\max_{p \subset B_{r+2}(x)} |1 - U_p| < \varepsilon$, $L > 4 r + 12$). For any fixed site $x$, let $d_x(y) := \min(|y_0 - x_0|_{\text{tor}}, r) + \min(|y_1 - x_1|_{\text{tor}}, r)$ (1-Lipschitz on $\Lambda$, and equal to $|y - x|_1$ identically on $B_r(x)$). For $\gamma \in [0, 1/2]$, the tilted operator $M_\gamma := e^{\gamma d_x} M e^{-\gamma d_x} = M + \delta_\gamma M$ has zero diagonal perturbation, nearest-neighbor perturbation $\le 4 (e^\gamma - 1) \le 4 e^{1/2} \gamma$ per neighbor, and corner perturbation $\le 2(e^{2\gamma} - 1) \le 4 e \gamma$ per corner. By Schur's test on the block row/column sums,
$$\|\delta_\gamma M\|_{\text{op}} \;\le\; \big(16 e^{1/2} + 16 e\big)\gamma \;=:\; C_H \gamma \qquad \big(\gamma \in [0, 1/2],\; C_H \approx 69.872\big).$$
Setting $\gamma_0 := \min\!\big(1/2,\, c_{\text{PV}}^2 / (2 C_H)\big) > 0$, we obtain uniformly for all $t \ge 0$, $k_5 \in [-\pi, \pi]$, and $x, y$:
$$\big\|(M + t)^{-1}_{x, y}\big\|_{\text{op}} \;\le\; \frac{2}{c_{\text{PV}}^2 + t} e^{-\gamma_0 d_x(y)}.$$

Fix the localization radius and fifth-dimension extent (for even $L$ large enough that $L > 4 r_0 + 12$):
$$r_0 := \left\lceil \frac{7}{\gamma_0} \ln L \right\rceil, \qquad L_5 := \left\lceil \frac{5}{\mu_T} \ln L \right\rceil.$$

**Lemma 2a (Dirichlet Schur identity & explicit local-gauge resolvent trace bound).** *Define the explicit constant*
$$C_3(\mu_0) := \frac{64 e^{\gamma_0}}{c_{\text{PV}}^2} + \frac{1824 e^{2\gamma_0}}{c_{\text{PV}}^4}$$
*(independent of $L, L_5, k_5, Q, \phi$). Then for all even $L > 4 r_0 + 12$ and every $k_5 \in [-\pi, \pi]$:*
1. *(Full-torus Dirichlet-to-torus localization)* For every $U \in G$ and **every site $x \in \Lambda$** (including cut-strip sites in $S_{\text{cut}}$), writing $B := B_{r_0}(x)$ and $M_{k_5}^{(B)}[U] := P_B M_{k_5}[U] P_B$,
   $$\Big|\operatorname{tr}_{2\times 2}\!\Big(\big(\ln M_{k_5}[U]\big)_{x, x} - \big(\ln M_{k_5}^{(B)}[U]\big)_{x, x}\Big)\Big| \;\le\; \frac{896 r_0}{c_{\text{PV}}^2} e^{-2\gamma_0 r_0} \;=\; O(L^{-13}).$$
   *Moreover, whenever $B_{r_0 + 2}(x) \subset \Lambda_+$ (resp. $\Lambda_-$), $M_{k_5}^{(B)}[U]$ depends exclusively on links in $U_+$ (resp. $U_-$), and its spectral gap and Combes–Thomas bound require only $\max_{p \subset \Lambda_+} |1 - U_p| < \varepsilon$ (resp. $\Lambda_-$).*

   - For every $U$ with $\max_{p \subset B_{r_0 + 2}(x)} |1 - U_p| < \varepsilon$ (in particular, for every $U_+$ with $\max_{p \subset \Lambda_+} |1 - U_p| < \varepsilon$ and $B_{r_0 + 2}(x) \subset \Lambda_+$),
     $$\Big|\operatorname{tr}_{2\times 2}\!\Big(\big(\ln M_{k_5}^{(B)}[U]\big)_{x, x} - \big(\ln M_{k_5}^{(B)}[U_{\text{AP}}]\big)_{x, x}\Big)\Big| \;\le\; C_3(\mu_0) \sum_{l \in E_{\text{sp}}(B)} e^{-2\gamma_0 d(x, l)} \big(a_l^{(x)}\big)^2.$$
   - Consequently, for every $U \in G$ and **every site $x \in \Lambda$**,
     $$\Big|\operatorname{tr}_{2\times 2}\!\Big(\big(\ln M_{k_5}[U]\big)_{x, x} - \big(\ln M_{k_5}[U_{\text{AP}}]\big)_{x, x}\Big)\Big| \;\le\; C_3(\mu_0) \sum_{l \in E_{\text{sp}}(B)} e^{-2\gamma_0 d(x, l)} \big(a_l^{(x)}\big)^2 + \frac{1792 r_0}{c_{\text{PV}}^2} e^{-2\gamma_0 r_0}.$$

**Proof.**
1. Since $U \in G$ and $L > 4 r_0 + 12$, every $x \in \Lambda$ has $\max_{p \subset B_{r_0 + 2}(x)} |1 - U_p| < \varepsilon$, so Lemma 2.0 gives $M := M_{k_5}[U] \succeq c_{\text{PV}}^2 I$ and $M_{BB} := M_{k_5}^{(B)}[U] \succeq c_{\text{PV}}^2 I$. Write $\ln A = \int_0^\infty ((1 + t)^{-1} I - (A + t)^{-1})\, dt$ for both $M$ and $M_{BB}$; the $(1+t)^{-1} I_2$ cancels identically in the $(x, x)$ block. Blocking $M + t = \begin{pmatrix} M_{BB} + t & M_{B\bar B} \\ M_{\bar B B} & M_{\bar B \bar B} + t \end{pmatrix}$ gives the exact Schur identity
   $$(M + t)^{-1}_{BB} - (M_{BB} + t)^{-1} = -(M_{BB} + t)^{-1} M_{B\bar B} (M + t)^{-1}_{\bar B B}.$$
   Since $M_{B\bar B}$ is supported on boundary sites $y \in \partial B$ ($\|y - x\|_\infty = r_0$, at most $8 r_0$ sites) coupled to $z \notin B$ ($\|z - x\|_\infty = r_0 + 1$), with $\sum_{z \notin B} \|M_{y, z}\|_{\text{op}} \le 2 \cdot 4 + 3 \cdot 2 = 14$ per site $y$ and $d_x(y) \ge r_0$, $d_x(z) \ge r_0$, Combes–Thomas on both $(M_{BB} + t)^{-1}_{x, y}$ and $(M + t)^{-1}_{z, x}$ yields
   $$\Big|\operatorname{tr}_{2\times 2}\!\Big(\big(\ln M\big)_{x, x} - \big(\ln M_{BB}\big)_{x, x}\Big)\Big| \;\le\; 2 \int_0^\infty \frac{4 \cdot (8 r_0 \cdot 14)}{(c_{\text{PV}}^2 + t)^2} e^{-2\gamma_0 r_0}\, dt = \frac{896 r_0}{c_{\text{PV}}^2} e^{-2\gamma_0 r_0} = O(L^{-13}).$$
2. For the Dirichlet-ball bound on $B = B_{r_0}(x)$ ($L > 4 r_0 + 12$ ensures $B$ does not wrap around the torus and is simply connected): gauge transformations act on $M_{k_5}^{(B)} = P_B M_{k_5} P_B$ by diagonal unitary conjugation $P_B D_g P_B$, leaving the $(x, x)$ block strictly invariant. Since $U^{(x)}$ has identical plaquette holonomies to $U$ on $B$ (any AP time seam inside $B$ has trivial plaquette curvature $(-1)(-1) = +1$ and is removed as pure gauge, while $U_{\text{AP}}|_B$ has $U_p \equiv 1$ and maps to $U^{(x)} \equiv 1$), we have $(\ln M_{k_5}^{(B)}[U])_{x, x} = (\ln M_1)_{x, x}$ and $(\ln M_{k_5}^{(B)}[U_{\text{AP}}])_{x, x} = (\ln M_0)_{x, x}$, where $M_0 := M_{k_5}^{(B)}[1]$ and $M_1 := M_{k_5}^{(B)}[U^{(x)}]$. Put $R_j(t) := (M_j + t)^{-1}$ and $\Delta M := M_1 - M_0 = \Delta^{(1)} M + \Delta^{(2)} M$ (splitting into the part linear in $a^{(x)}$ and the quadratic-and-higher remainder), and use the exact symmetric second-order resolvent identity
   $$R_0(t) - R_1(t) = R_0(t) \Delta M R_0(t) - R_0(t) \Delta M R_1(t) \Delta M R_0(t).$$
   - **Site-by-site charge-conjugation vanishing of the linear trace** (incorporating Refuter Row 5's corrected identity, verified to `5.3e-16` for $M_{BB}$ and `1.6e-15` for $M$): with $\mathcal{C} = \sigma_2$, $\mathcal{C} D_W[U]^T \mathcal{C}^{-1} = D_W[U^*]$, hence $\mathcal{C} H_W[U]^T \mathcal{C}^{-1} = -D_W[U^*]\gamma_5 = -\gamma_5 H_W[U^*] \gamma_5$ and
     $$\mathcal{C} M[U]^T \mathcal{C}^{-1} = \gamma_5 M[U^*] \gamma_5$$

Taking the $2 \times 2$ trace at $(x, x)$:
     $$\operatorname{tr}_{2\times 2}(A_{x, x}) = \operatorname{tr}_{2\times 2}(\mathcal{C} (A^T)_{x, x} \mathcal{C}^{-1}) = -\operatorname{tr}_{2\times 2}(\gamma_5 A_{x, x} \gamma_5) = -\operatorname{tr}_{2\times 2}(A_{x, x}) \implies \operatorname{tr}_{2\times 2}(A_{x, x}) \equiv 0$$
     (verified to `2.06e-12` across all sites of $B$, including boundary sites!).
   - **Term 1 quadratic remainder ($\Delta^{(2)} M$):** In local axial gauge ($a_0 \equiv 0$), $(\Delta M)_{v, v} = 0$, nearest-neighbor spatial entries ($|v - u|_1 = 1$, link $l = (v, u)$) satisfy $\|(\Delta M)_{v, u}\|_{\text{op}} \le 4 |a_l|$ and $\|(\Delta^{(2)} M)_{v, u}\|_{\text{op}} \le 2 a_l^2$, and each diagonal-corner entry ($u = v + s_0 e_0 + s_1 e_1$) uses one spatial link $l_1$ incident to $v$ and one $l_2$ incident to $v + s_0 e_0$, with $\|(\Delta M)_{v, u}\|_{\text{op}} \le |a_{l_1}| + |a_{l_2}|$ and $\|(\Delta^{(2)} M)_{v, u}\|_{\text{op}} \le \frac{1}{2}(a_{l_1}^2 + a_{l_2}^2)$. Applying Combes–Thomas on $R_0(t)$ and integrating $\int_0^\infty \frac{4\, dt}{(c_{\text{PV}}^2 + t)^2} = \frac{4}{c_{\text{PV}}^2}$ gives
     $$\int_0^\infty \Big|\operatorname{tr}_{2\times 2}\!\big((R_0(t) \Delta^{(2)} M R_0(t))_{x, x}\big)\Big|\, dt \;\le\; \frac{64 e^{\gamma_0}}{c_{\text{PV}}^2} \sum_{l \in E_{\text{sp}}(B)} e^{-2\gamma_0 d(x, l)} a_l^2.$$
   - **Term 2 (exact Hilbert-space bound — single sum, exact $e^{-2\gamma_0 d}$ decay):** Let $E_x : \mathbb{C}^2 \to \ell^2(B; \mathbb{C}^2)$ be the site-$x$ inclusion and $\Psi(t) := \Delta M R_0(t) E_x$.      $$\sum_{v \in B} \|\Psi(t)_v\|_{\text{op}}^2 \;\le\; \frac{6(2 \cdot 144 e^{2\gamma_0} + 4 \cdot 4)}{(c_{\text{PV}}^2 + t)^2} \sum_{l \in E_{\text{sp}}(B)} e^{-2\gamma_0 d(x, l)} a_l^2 \;\le\; \frac{1824 e^{2\gamma_0}}{(c_{\text{PV}}^2 + t)^2} \sum_{l \in E_{\text{sp}}(B)} e^{-2\gamma_0 d(x, l)} a_l^2.$$
     Multiplying by $\frac{2}{c_{\text{PV}}^2 + t}$ and integrating $\int_0^\infty \frac{2\, dt}{(c_{\text{PV}}^2 + t)^3} = \frac{1}{c_{\text{PV}}^4}$ yields $\frac{1824 e^{2\gamma_0}}{c_{\text{PV}}^4} \sum_{l \in E_{\text{sp}}(B)} e^{-2\gamma_0 d(x, l)} a_l^2$. Adding Term 1 and Term 2 proves the Dirichlet-ball bound with $C_3(\mu_0) = \frac{64 e^{\gamma_0}}{c_{\text{PV}}^2} + \frac{1824 e^{2\gamma_0}}{c_{\text{PV}}^4}$. Finally, for every $U \in G$ and every $x \in \Lambda$, applying Part 1 to both $U$ and $U_{\text{AP}} \in G$ yields the full-torus bound with tail $2 \cdot \frac{896 r_0}{c_{\text{PV}}^2} e^{-2\gamma_0 r_0} = \frac{1792 r_0}{c_{\text{PV}}^2} e^{-2\gamma_0 r_0}$. $\blacksquare$

## 4. Lemma 3 — Exact Plaquette-Dependent 2D Character Expansion & Strip Tail Bound

**Lemma 3.0 (1-line Vandermonde–Turán Bessel decay).** *For all $x > 0$ and all integers $n \ge 1$, $I_n(x)^2 > I_{n-1}(x) I_{n+1}(x)$, and for all $n \ge 2$,*
$$\frac{I_n(x)}{I_0(x)} \;\le\; \exp\!\left(-\frac{(n - 1)^2}{2(x + n)}\right).$$

**Proof.** In the power series $I_n(x) = \sum_{j=0}^\infty \frac{(x/2)^{n+2j}}{j!(n+j)!}$, the coefficient of $(x/2)^{2n+2m}$ in $I_n(x)^2 - I_{n-1}(x) I_{n+1}(x)$ is, by Vandermonde's identity $\sum_{j=0}^m \binom{m}{j}\binom{2n+m}{n+j} = \binom{2n+2m}{n+m}$,
$$\frac{1}{m!(2n+m)!}\left[\binom{2n+2m}{n+m} - \binom{2n+2m}{n+m-1}\right] = \frac{1}{m!(2n+m)!(n+m+1)}\binom{2n+2m}{n+m} > 0,$$
proving $I_n(x)^2 > I_{n-1}(x) I_{n+1}(x)$ term-by-term! Thus $r_k := I_k(x) / I_{k-1}(x) \in (0, 1)$ is strictly decreasing in $k \ge 1$. Substituting $r_{k-1} > r_k$ into the exact Bessel recurrence $\frac{1}{r_{k-1}} - r_k = \frac{2(k-1)}{x}$ ($k \ge 2$) yields $\frac{1}{r_k} - r_k > \frac{2(k-1)}{x}$, so
$$r_k < \frac{x}{(k - 1) + \sqrt{(k - 1)^2 + x^2}} = \exp\!\left(-\operatorname{arsinh}\frac{k - 1}{x}\right) \le \exp\!\left(-\frac{k - 1}{x + k - 1}\right) \qquad (k \ge 2)$$
(since $\operatorname{arsinh}(u) = \int_0^u (1 + s^2)^{-1/2}\, ds \ge \frac{u}{\sqrt{1 + u^2}} \ge \frac{u}{1 + u}$ for all $u \ge 0$). Multiplying over $k = 2, \dots, n$ (with $r_1 < 1$) and using $\sum_{k=2}^n (k - 1) = \frac{n(n-1)}{2} \ge \frac{(n-1)^2}{2}$ gives $I_n(x)/I_0(x) \le \exp(-\frac{(n-1)^2}{2(x+n)})$. $\blacksquare$

**Lemma 3 (Exact global action cap & cut-strip action cap).** *For all sufficiently large $L$:*
1. *(Global action cap — Lemma 3a)* With $K = 4(c_f(H_T) + 1)$, $\mu_W(\neg G_{\text{act}}) \le e^{-(c_f(H_T) + 1) V}$.
2. *(Cut-strip tail)* Let $E_{\text{strip}} := \{|\Delta_{\text{cut}}[U]| \le L^{-3/4}\}$, and set the explicit constant $c_* := \frac{\mu_T}{144(e\ell)^2 C_{\text{PV}}(\mu_0)} > 0$. Then for all sufficiently large $L$,
   $$\mu_W\big((G \cap G_{\text{act}}) \setminus E_{\text{strip}}\big) \;\le\; \exp\!\left(-2 c_*\, \frac{L^{5/4}}{\ln L}\right).$$

**Proof.** For any subset of plaquettes $S \subseteq \Lambda$ and any $\alpha \in (0, 1)$, expanding $e^{\beta_p \cos\theta_p} = \sum_{n_p \in \mathbb{Z}} I_{n_p}(\beta_p) e^{i n_p \theta_p}$ with $\beta_p = (1 - \alpha)\beta$ on $S$ and $\beta_p = \beta$ on $\Lambda \setminus S$ and integrating every link $U_l$ forces $n_p \equiv n$ across the 2D torus, yielding the **exact** identity (verified vs. Monte Carlo in `verify_lane2_route5_bundle.py`):
$$\mathbb{E}_W\!\left[\exp\!\Big(\alpha \beta \sum_{p \in S} (1 - \cos\theta_p)\Big)\right] = \frac{\sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \operatorname{ive}\big(n, (1-\alpha)\beta\big)^{|S|} \operatorname{ive}(n, \beta)^{V - |S|}}{\sum_{n \in \mathbb{Z}} \operatorname{ive}(n, \beta)^V},$$
where $\operatorname{ive}(n, x) := e^{-x} I_n(x) > 0$. Take $\alpha = 1/2$:
- For **Part 1** ($S = \Lambda$, $|S| = V$): $\frac{\operatorname{ive}(0, \beta/2)}{\operatorname{ive}(0, \beta)} = \sqrt{2}(1 + O(\beta^{-1}))$, and by Lemma 3.0 the $n \ne 0$ sum adds at most a constant factor $C_W(e\ell)$ (since $(|n|-1)^2 V / (2(\beta/2 + |n|)) = (e\ell)^2 (|n|-1)^2 / (1 + 2|n|/\beta)$), giving $\mathbb{E}_W[e^{\frac{\beta}{2} \sum_p (1 - \cos\theta_p)}] \le C_W 2^{V/2}(1 + O(\beta^{-1}))^V \le e^{0.4 V}$. Markov at $K(e\ell)^2$ yields $\mu_W(\neg G_{\text{act}}) \le e^{-\frac{1}{2} K V + 0.4 V} \le e^{-(c_f(H_T) + 1) V}$.
- For **Part 2** ($S = S_{\text{cut}}^+$, $|S| = O(L \ln L) \le V/2$): the denominator is $\ge \operatorname{ive}(0, \beta)^V$. For $n = 0$, the ratio is $(\operatorname{ive}(0, \beta/2)/\operatorname{ive}(0, \beta))^{|S|} \le 2^{|S|} = e^{(\ln 2) |S|}$; for $|n| \ge 1$, since $V - |S| \ge V/2$, Lemma 3.0 bounds the $n \ne 0$ sum by $C_W'(e\ell)$ times the $n = 0$ term. Thus
  $$\mathbb{E}_W\!\left[\exp\!\Big(\frac{\beta}{2} \sum_{p \in S_{\text{cut}}^+} (1 - \cos\theta_p)\Big)\right] \;\le\; C_W'(e\ell)\, e^{(\ln 2)|S_{\text{cut}}^+|}.$$
  On $(G \cap G_{\text{act}}) \setminus E_{\text{strip}}$, Corollary 2b(4) gives $\frac{1}{2} L_5 C_{\text{PV}}(\mu_0) \sum_{p \in S_{\text{cut}}^+} \theta_p^2 \ge \frac{1}{2} L^{-3/4}$ (for large $L$), and on $G$, $1 - \cos\theta_p \ge \frac{1}{3} \theta_p^2$ (since $|\theta_p| \le \theta_\varepsilon < \pi/3$). Applying Markov with $\frac{\beta}{2} = \frac{L^2}{2(e\ell)^2}$ yields
  $$\mu_W\big((G \cap G_{\text{act}}) \setminus E_{\text{strip}}\big) \;\le\; C_W'(e\ell) \exp\!\left(-\frac{\mu_T L^{5/4}}{36(e\ell)^2 C_{\text{PV}}(\mu_0) \ln L} + (\ln 2)|S_{\text{cut}}^+|\right) \;\le\; \exp\!\left(-2 c_* \frac{L^{5/4}}{\ln L}\right)$$
  for all sufficiently large $L$, with $c_* := \frac{\mu_T}{144(e\ell)^2 C_{\text{PV}}(\mu_0)} > 0$. $\blacksquare$

## 5. Proof of Main Theorem (`T1-OS'`, Route (v)) — 4-Piece Assembly

First record the two elementary free-torus & Lane 1 bounds on $W_{\text{ov}}[U]$:
1. **Free AP torus Riemann-sum bound:** $\big|\ln \det D_{\text{ov}}^{H_T, (m_f)}[U_{\text{AP}}] + c_f(H_T) V\big| \le C_{\text{AP}} L$.
   *(Proof: On $U_{\text{AP}}$, $k \in \text{BZ}_{\text{AP}}$ is the midpoint grid of spacing $2\pi/L$ with $|k| \ge \sqrt{2}\pi/L$. First, $0 \le \ln((1-m_f^2)|\lambda_0|^2 + m_f^2) - \ln|\lambda_0|^2 \le m_f^2/|\lambda_0|^2$ sums to $O(\ln L)$, while $V \ln(1 - m_f^2) = O(1)$. Second, $|\nabla \ln|\lambda_0(q)|^2| \le C/|q|$ bounds the midpoint-rule error on cells $|k| \ge 2\pi/L$ by $\sum_k \frac{C}{L|k|} = O(L)$, and the 4 origin-adjacent cells contribute $O(\ln L)$.)*
2. **Global upper bound & Lane 1 lower bound:** Since $D_{\text{ov}}^{H_T, (m_f)}[U]$ is normal with spectrum on $|z - \frac{1+m_f}{2}| = \frac{1-m_f}{2}$, $0 < \det D_{\text{ov}}^{H_T, (m_f)}[U] \le 1$ everywhere on $U(1)^{2V}$ (a.e. under $\mu_W$), so $W_{\text{ov}}[U] \le \exp(c_f(H_T) V + C_{\text{AP}} L)$ everywhere. Meanwhile, **Clawd's Lane 1 Lemma Z** (`LANE1-Z-LEMMA.md`, Section 8 for $H_T$ at fixed physical mass $m_f = \mu\ell/L$, signed off in Letter 89 and refereed in Letter 295 with $L_0(H_T) = 32$) gives $\ln \mathbb{E}_W[\det D_{\text{ov}}^{H_T, (m_f)}] \ge -c_f(H_T) V - O(L (\ln L)^{3/2})$ for every fixed $e\ell > 0$, hence
   $$\mathbb{E}_W[W_{\text{ov}}] \;=\; \frac{\mathbb{E}_W[\det D_{\text{ov}}^{H_T, (m_f)}]}{\det D_{\text{ov}}^{H_T, (m_f)}[U_{\text{AP}}]} \;\ge\; \exp\!\Big(-C_Z L (\ln L)^{3/2}\Big).$$

Now partition $U(1)^{2V}$ into four disjoint regions and bound $\eta_L := \frac{\mathbb{E}_W[|W_{\text{ov}} - W_{\text{aux}}|]}{\mathbb{E}_W[W_{\text{ov}}]}$:

- **Piece A (Good cut strip $G \cap G_{\text{act}} \cap E_{\text{strip}}$):** By Corollary 2b(4), $|\ln W_{\text{aux}} - \ln W_{\text{ov}}| \le L^{-3/4} + O(L^{-2}) \le \frac{3}{2} L^{-3/4}$, so $|W_{\text{ov}} - W_{\text{aux}}| \le 2 L^{-3/4} W_{\text{ov}}$. Therefore $W_{\text{ov}}$ **cancels identically**:
  $$\frac{\mathbb{E}_W\big[|W_{\text{ov}} - W_{\text{aux}}|\, \mathbf{1}_{G \cap G_{\text{act}} \cap E_{\text{strip}}}\big]}{\mathbb{E}_W[W_{\text{ov}}]} \;\le\; 2 L^{-3/4}\, \frac{\mathbb{E}_W[W_{\text{ov}} \mathbf{1}_{G \cap G_{\text{act}} \cap E_{\text{strip}}}]}{\mathbb{E}_W[W_{\text{ov}}]} \;\le\; 2 L^{-3/4}.$$

- **Piece B (Rare cut-strip spike $(G \cap G_{\text{act}}) \setminus E_{\text{strip}}$):** By Corollary 2b(2, 3), $|W_{\text{ov}} - W_{\text{aux}}| \le 4 L^{C_0}$. By Lemma 3(2) and Lane 1's lower bound on $\mathbb{E}_W[W_{\text{ov}}]$:
  $$\frac{\mathbb{E}_W\big[|W_{\text{ov}} - W_{\text{aux}}|\, \mathbf{1}_{(G \cap G_{\text{act}}) \setminus E_{\text{strip}}}\big]}{\mathbb{E}_W[W_{\text{ov}}]} \;\le\; 4 L^{C_0} \exp\!\left(-2 c_* \frac{L^{5/4}}{\ln L} + C_Z L (\ln L)^{3/2}\right) \;\le\; \frac{1}{2} \exp\!\left(-c_*\, \frac{L^{5/4}}{\ln L}\right)$$
  (for large $L$, since $L^{5/4}/\ln L \gg L (\ln L)^{3/2}$!).

- **Piece C (Action-cap complement $G \setminus G_{\text{act}}$):** Here $W_{\text{ov}} \le \exp(c_f(H_T) V + C_{\text{AP}} L)$ and $W_{\text{aux}} \le L^{C_0}$ (Corollary 2b(2)). By Lemma 3(1), $\mu_W(\neg G_{\text{act}}) \le e^{-(c_f(H_T) + 1) V}$, so
  $$\frac{\mathbb{E}_W\big[|W_{\text{ov}} - W_{\text{aux}}|\, \mathbf{1}_{G \setminus G_{\text{act}}}\big]}{\mathbb{E}_W[W_{\text{ov}}]} \;\le\; 2 \exp\!\Big(-V + C_{\text{AP}} L + C_Z L (\ln L)^{3/2}\Big) \;=\; e^{-\Omega(L^2)}.$$

- **Piece D (Inadmissible set $\neg G$):** Here $W_{\text{ov}} \le \exp(c_f(H_T) V + C_{\text{AP}} L)$ and $W_{\text{aux}} \le L^{C_0}$ (Corollary 2b(2) holds *everywhere*, including on $(G_+^{\text{cap}} \cap G_-^{\text{cap}}) \setminus G$!). By Lane 1 eq. (1.1), $\mu_W(\neg G) \le C' V^2 \exp(-\frac{\varepsilon^2}{2(e\ell)^2} V)$ (using $1 - \cos\theta_\varepsilon = \varepsilon^2 / 2$). Since $e\ell < e\ell_0 = \varepsilon / \sqrt{2 c_f(H_T)}$, $\delta_0 := \frac{\varepsilon^2}{2(e\ell)^2} - c_f(H_T) > 0$, giving
  $$\frac{\mathbb{E}_W\big[|W_{\text{ov}} - W_{\text{aux}}|\, \mathbf{1}_{\neg G}\big]}{\mathbb{E}_W[W_{\text{ov}}]} \;\le\; C'' V^2 \exp\!\Big(-\delta_0 V + C_{\text{AP}} L + C_Z L (\ln L)^{3/2}\Big) \;=\; e^{-\Omega(L^2)}.$$

Summing Pieces A–D (with Pieces C + D $\le \frac{1}{2} \exp(-c_* L^{5/4}/\ln L)$ for large $L$) yields $\eta_L \le 2 L^{-3/4} + \exp(-c_* L^{5/4}/\ln L) < 1$ for all sufficiently large $L$. In particular $\mathbb{E}_W[W_{\text{aux}}] \ge (1 - \eta_L) \mathbb{E}_W[W_{\text{ov}}] > 0$, and the elementary triangle inequality gives
$$d_{\text{TV}}\!\left(\mu_{\text{ov}}^{(L)},\, \mu_{\text{aux}}^{(L)}\right) = \frac{1}{2} \mathbb{E}_W\!\left[\left|\frac{W_{\text{ov}}}{\mathbb{E}_W W_{\text{ov}}} - \frac{W_{\text{aux}}}{\mathbb{E}_W W_{\text{aux}}}\right|\right] \le \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{\mathbb{E}_W[|W_{\text{ov}} - W_{\text{aux}}|]}{\mathbb{E}_W W_{\text{ov}}} + \left|1 - \frac{\mathbb{E}_W W_{\text{aux}}}{\mathbb{E}_W W_{\text{ov}}}\right|\right) \le \eta_L = O\!\big(L^{-3/4}\big).$$
Finally, since $\mathbb{E}_{\text{aux}}^{(L)}[(\Theta F) F] \ge 0$ (Corollary 2b(1)) and $|(\Theta F)(U) F(U)| \le \|F\|_\infty^2$,
$$\mathbb{E}_{\text{ov}}^{(L)}\!\big[(\Theta F) F\big] \;\ge\; \mathbb{E}_{\text{aux}}^{(L)}\!\big[(\Theta F) F\big] - 2\, d_{\text{TV}}\!\left(\mu_{\text{ov}}^{(L)},\, \mu_{\text{aux}}^{(L)}\right) \|F\|_\infty^2 \;\ge\; -2\, d_{\text{TV}}\!\left(\mu_{\text{ov}}^{(L)},\, \mu_{\text{aux}}^{(L)}\right) \|F\|_\infty^2 \;=\; -O\!\big(L^{-3/4}\big) \|F\|_\infty^2. \qquad \blacksquare$$
