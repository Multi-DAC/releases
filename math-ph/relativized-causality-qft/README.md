# Relativized Causality in Quantum Field Theory: Sorkin Signaling for Lüders Channels and Multi-Region Non-Signaling for PSNI Operations

**Primary classification:** `math-ph`
**Cross-lists:** `quant-ph`, `hep-th`

## Plain-language statement of the result

In relativistic quantum field theory (QFT), the spectrum condition rules out sharp (projection-valued) translation-covariant spacetime reference frames. Fewster and Loveridge (`arXiv:2611.21768v1`) showed that relativizing a QFT against a realistic quantum reference frame (QRF) replaces exact microcausality by rapid $O(L^{-\infty})$ commutator decay and $O(L^{-\infty})$ two-region non-disturbance for operations with finite almost-local Kraus decompositions (including Lüders channels of finite-outcome relativized local observables), while leaving open (Abstract, Sec. 4, Sec. 5) whether operational causality survives in protocols with three or more regions—specifically, whether relativization excludes Sorkin-type impossible-measurement obstructions.

This paper establishes a **split answer**:

1. **Relativized Lüders channels signal at $O(1)$ (`Theorem 3.1`, `Proposition 3.3`).** On the free real scalar field in Minkowski space ($m > 0, d \ge 2$ or $m = 0, d \ge 3$), for *every* nonzero $L^1$ frame localization density $\rho_\psi$ (and fixed local measurement contrast $(\alpha, \lambda)$), **both** the effect-first relativized Lüders channel of `2611.21768` Cor. 4.7 and the channel-first mixture of translated Lüders channels still signal in a three-region Sorkin protocol by a strictly positive $O(1)$ amount that does not decay with the causal separation $L \to \infty$. The negative direction is the expected one; what is proved is universality across every physical frame state, uniformity in $L$, the explicit $O(\lambda^2 \alpha^8)$ dependence on the shape of $\rho_\psi$ (with Cauchy–Schwarz hierarchy $|S_2^{\mathrm{eff}}| \le |S_2^{\mathrm{mix}}|$), and a massive no-sharpening obstruction (`Proposition 3.3`) showing that for $m > 0$ one cannot reduce the theorem to rescaling an absolute Sorkin scenario.
2. **Relativized PSNI channels do not signal (`Lemma 4.1`, `Theorem 4.2`).** For any translation-covariant AQFT net on Minkowski space, operations satisfying Jubb's past-support-non-increasing (PSNI) criterion—in particular, all Fewster–Verch probe-induced operations and all causal Weyl channels of Mandrysch, Simmons, and Navascués (`arXiv:2607.12976`)—preserve multi-region causality under channel-first relativization: exactly under a shared QRF (Jubb's criterion plus translation covariance), and up to an explicit, operation-independent pairwise autocorrelation tail bound ($O(L^{-\infty})$ for Schwartz frame densities $\rho_\psi \in \mathcal{S}(M)$) under independent frame restrictions. A self-contained $n$-node Minkowski causal-topology lemma (`Lemma 4.1`, requiring no Cauchy-surface foliation and constructing genuine regions with finitely many connected components for arbitrary compact interaction zones) extends this bound to arbitrary causally ordered $n$-region networks without feed-forward (whereas a causally ordered network of causal Weyl instruments with classical feed-forward, treated as a single composed PSNI channel with interaction zone $O_2 := \bigcup_{j=2}^n O_j$ via `2607.12976` Thm. 20 and relativized as a whole, falls under `Theorem 4.2(a)–(b)`).
3. **Infinite almost-local Kraus boundary & finite-dimensional square-root obstruction (`Proposition 5.1`, `Proposition A.1`).** Section 5 clarifies the infinite-Kraus boundary of `2611.21768` Def. 4.3 & Prop. 4.4(ii) (without contradicting anything stated there): countable almost-local Kraus families without uniformity can fail two-region $O(L^{-\infty})$ non-disturbance whenever $\eta_m$ is not $O(L_m^{-\infty})$, whereas uniform Stinespring column-norm almost-locality $\varepsilon_L = O(L^{-\infty})$ restores it with the sharp estimate $2\|B - B_L\| + 2\|B_L\|\varepsilon_L$. Appendix A records a purely finite-dimensional non-commutative square-root obstruction (`Proposition A.1`) closing the generic operator-norm proof route from effect approximation $\sum_i (E_i - B_i) \le \delta\,\mathrm{id}$ to Lüders Kraus approximation, while noting explicitly that its rank-one $B_i$ carry no convolution structure and make no assertion about relativized QFT effects themselves.
